# Frontline-up removal; gaps remain

A complete line is eligible only after every line below it is resolved, and removals leave empty space instead of pulling the board down. That keeps Shot trajectories and the Continuous Stream predictable, and makes the consecutive-gap pattern a Cascade instead of two independent clears.

Clearing any complete line, or applying local gravity after a removal, were rejected: they skip the Frontline and make Shot timing depend on the board compacting.
