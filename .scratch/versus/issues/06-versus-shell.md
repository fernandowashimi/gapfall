# Versus shell: Esc, Settings, forfeit

Type: grilling
Blocked by: 03

## Question

Versus cannot pause. Single Player still pauses on Esc and tab blur, and Settings is reachable from the Main Menu and from pause. What does the app shell do in Versus?

Grill, one question at a time, after [Main Menu and matchmaking UX](03-menu-matchmaking-ux.md) has locked the menu/queue flow:

- Esc during a Versus Round: no-op, or forfeit?
- Tab blur during Versus: the board keeps simulating (locked) — is there any UI, or silence?
- Settings during Versus: unreachable, or reachable without pausing?
- Is there a forfeit action besides closing the tab (a button, Esc, abandon to Main Menu)?
- Choosing Main Menu from Versus game-over vs abandoning mid-Round: both a forfeit if the opponent is still playing?

Recommend: Esc is a no-op during Versus play (no pause, no accidental forfeit); the only deliberate forfeit besides closing the tab is an abandon-to-Main-Menu control that is obvious; Settings stays Main Menu only in Versus; mid-Round abandon is a forfeit; game-over Main Menu is just leaving after the Match has already ended.
